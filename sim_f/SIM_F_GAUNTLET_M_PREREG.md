1. Research pass: collect LLM escape/jailbreak/prompt-injection/agent-attack research and
   real-world incident write-ups published since the last cycle (arXiv, vendor security
   blogs, AI-safety preprints, disclosed attack frameworks).
2. Selection rule (deterministic, bounded): at most 3 new waves per cycle, one per attack
   class where available — impersonated authority, tool-invocation inducement, governance
   mutation, exfiltration, chaos exploitation, plus any genuinely novel class. Every new
   wave MUST carry a public source URL + retrieval date in the prereg.
3. Freeze: the cycle corpus (core waves + new waves) is committed with a sha BEFORE any
   caged seat is created. No wave is added or edited after freeze.
